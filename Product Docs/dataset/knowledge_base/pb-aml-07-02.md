# Review relationship between sending and receiving accounts

Check whether a documented or previously observed relationship exists between the customer and the counterparty named on the transaction. Look for prior transactions, a declared business relationship, or a known family connection. An unverified, first-time counterparty for a large-value transaction is a relevant factor to weigh alongside other evidence, not a conclusion on its own — many legitimate first-time payments exist.
