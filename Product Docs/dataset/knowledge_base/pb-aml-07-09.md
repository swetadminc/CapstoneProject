# Handling of external or other-bank accounts

When a counterparty account belongs to another bank, KYC information and transaction history for that account are not available to this investigation. Record this explicitly as Missing evidence rather than assuming elevated or reduced risk in either direction. Note that the absence of data is itself a normal, expected condition for external accounts, not an anomaly.
