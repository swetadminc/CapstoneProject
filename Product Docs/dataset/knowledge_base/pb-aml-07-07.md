# Escalation criteria for rapid-movement-of-funds scenario

Escalate to a senior investigator or compliance when: (a) source of funds remains undeclared after request, (b) the receiving counterparty cannot be identified or verified, and (c) no documented business relationship or explanation is on file. All three together warrant escalation; any one alone does not.
