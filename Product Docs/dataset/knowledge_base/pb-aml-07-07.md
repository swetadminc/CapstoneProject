# Escalation criteria for the rapid-movement-of-funds scenario

Escalate to a senior investigator or compliance when three conditions are all true: the source of funds remains undeclared after a documented request, the receiving counterparty cannot be identified or verified through any available record, and no documented business relationship or reasonable explanation is on file. Any single condition alone is not sufficient grounds for escalation under this playbook.
