# Documentation required before closing a velocity case

Before closing with no concern: confirm the transaction count and clustering were reported accurately, the frequency was compared against the customer's declared business type, any structuring-like pattern was explicitly checked for and ruled out or explained, and the investigator has recorded a written rationale.
