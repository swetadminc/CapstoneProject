# Distinguish an investigation indicator from a conclusion

An identified red flag is a reason to investigate further, not evidence of wrongdoing on its own. The investigation report must never assert that a transaction is illegal, criminal, or confirmed suspicious without a human investigator's review and sign-off. Findings should be phrased as observations with supporting evidence, leaving the assessment of intent to the investigator.
