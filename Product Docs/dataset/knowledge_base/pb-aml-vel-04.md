# Escalation criteria for the velocity scenario

Escalate when the transaction frequency is inconsistent with the customer's declared profile, AND no reasonable business explanation is provided, AND (if present) a structuring-like pattern is observed. Frequency alone, with a plausible explanation on file, does not meet this bar.
