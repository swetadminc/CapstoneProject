# Documentation required before case closure

Before closing a case with no concern identified, confirm four things are true: the customer's KYC status is current, the source of funds is either documented or reasonably explained, the counterparty relationship is understood well enough to account for the activity, and the investigator has recorded a written rationale for the decision in the case file.
