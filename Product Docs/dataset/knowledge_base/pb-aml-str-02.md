# Escalation criteria for a structuring pattern

Escalate when the transaction cluster is confirmed to be structuring-like (see PB-AML-STR-01), AND the
customer's declared business does not plausibly generate that specific pattern of repeated near-threshold
deposits, AND no reasonable explanation is on file after a documented request. A wholesale or cash-intensive
business receiving frequent moderate payments is not automatically suspicious — the escalation bar is the
combination of the pattern, the lack of a plausible business rationale, and the absence of an explanation,
not the pattern alone.
